# How to change the words on the website

This guide is for committee members. You don't need to know anything about code or websites. If you get stuck, ask Stephen. You can't break the live site: if something goes wrong, the last working version stays up.

## Before you start (once only)

1. Make a free account at [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup). Use an email address you check.
2. Send your GitHub username to Stephen. He'll add you to the website.
3. Look out for an email from GitHub inviting you, and press **Accept invitation**.

## Making a change

1. Go to [app.pagescms.org](https://app.pagescms.org) and press **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Choose **eaglesham-community**.
3. On the left you'll see:
   - **Home page**: the headline and the paragraph under it
   - **Pages**: The plan, About, Our view, Privacy, Accessibility
   - **How to respond page**: the steps, and the questions and answers at the bottom
   - **The three sites**: one entry for each site
   - **The big issues**: traffic, wildlife, infrastructure
   - **Response helper points**: the points residents can tick on "How to respond"
   - **Glossary**: planning terms explained
4. Open the page you want to change. Each box has a short hint underneath it saying what goes there.
5. Make your change, then press **Save**.

The website updates itself about two minutes later. Refresh the page in your browser to see it.

## Good habits

- **Write TODO** in any paragraph that still needs checking or finishing. It shows on the site with a dashed box around it, so nobody can miss it. Every TODO must be gone before the site launches.
- **Every fact needs a source.** Add it under **Sources** at the bottom of the page: who published it, the title and date, and the web address.
- **Change "Last updated"** to today's date when you change facts.
- **Use "Notes for the committee"** for reminders. Only editors see these notes. They never appear on the website.
- **To hide a page** that isn't ready, tick **Hide this page from the live site**.
- **Headings:** use "Heading 2" for each section of a page. Don't make text bold to look like a heading.

## Writing for the site

We're neighbours explaining something complicated, clearly and fairly.

- Short sentences and everyday words. Explain any planning term the first time you use it.
- Facts need a source. If we're not sure, we don't say it.
- Opinions belong on the "Our view" page, written as "We think…".
- Never criticise anyone's motives, and don't name individuals unless you're quoting something they said in public, with a source.
- Never say where badger setts or bat roosts are.
- No exclamation marks and no capitals for emphasis.

Full rules: [style guide, section 11](STYLE-GUIDE.md#11-writing-style).

## If the site doesn't change

Most likely a box was filled in a way the site can't use, for example a web address without `https://`. The site keeps showing the old version, and nothing breaks. Tell Stephen which page you changed and he'll sort it out.

## Things only Stephen can change (for now)

The site name, the deadline, the council's links and the contact email live in the site's code. Ask Stephen to change these.
