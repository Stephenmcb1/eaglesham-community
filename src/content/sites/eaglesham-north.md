## What's proposed

From the council's development framework and phasing plan:

- **About 360 homes** of mixed tenure, built in three phases:
  - Phase 1, 2028/29 to 2031/32: about 140 homes, plus about 34 hectares of enhanced strategic green network
  - Phase 2, 2032/33 to 2034/35: about 120 homes
  - Phase 3, 2035/36 to 2037/38: about 100 homes
- **A strategic green network** of about 34 hectares, with about 5 km of paths and active travel routes (LDP3 ref SP2.18)
- **A community, wellbeing and enterprise hub** (LP5.17) and **a village green / community space at a "village gateway"** (LP5.18)
- **An extension to Eaglesham Cemetery** (LP5.16)
- Community growing space and an orchard (LP5.19), and a neighbourhood play area (LP6.4)
- The framework drawing says to **protect the Weary Tree**, a local landmark, and its setting

The site covers 59 hectares on the council's map. All of it is green belt in the current plan (LDP2). In LDP3, about 31.5 hectares, mainly the green network, would stay green belt, and the rest would be released for housing.
