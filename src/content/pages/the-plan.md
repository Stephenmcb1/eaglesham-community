## What is LDP3?

A Local Development Plan decides where new homes, businesses and other development can go, and sets the policies the council uses to decide planning applications. Local Development Plan 3 (LDP3) will be East Renfrewshire's plan for 2027 to 2037. Together with Scotland's National Planning Framework 4 (NPF4), it will be the starting point for planning decisions across the area.

The Scottish Government has set a minimum number of homes East Renfrewshire must plan for. The council says this means LDP3 must include sites for at least 3,100 new homes over 10 years. It has named four growth areas: Barrhead, Busby, Clarkston and Eaglesham.

The plan also proposes raising the share of affordable homes required in new housing developments from 25% to 30%.

## What is a response?

A response, which the council calls a "representation", is your written view on the Proposed Plan, sent to the council during the consultation. You can support, object to or comment on any part of it. Only responses lodged with the council count. Social media posts, polls and petitions don't go forward to the examination.

## What counts

Planning decisions can only take account of "material considerations": things that are relevant in planning terms. Examples include:

- traffic, road safety and public transport
- effects on wildlife, trees and the green belt
- school places, health services, water and drainage
- flooding
- the character of the conservation village and its setting

Things that usually don't count include effects on property values, the loss of a private view, and who the developer is.
